# Obsidian Futures V5.88 — Scenario Lab

Scenario Lab analyzes completed V5.86 Simulator trades by recorded market regime. It separates Bull/Uptrend, Bear/Downtrend, Sideways/Ranging, High/Low Volatility when those labels are available, plus an All Trades baseline.

Safety:
- Simulation/analysis only.
- Does not create real orders.
- Does not modify Simulator balance or trade history.
- No synthetic future trades are generated.
- Small samples are explicitly marked as collecting/limited.

Export: Scenario Lab can export the scenario summary as JSON.
