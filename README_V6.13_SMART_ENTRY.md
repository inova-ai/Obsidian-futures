# Obsidian Futures V6.13 — Smart Entry Timing + Correlation Guard

V6.13 adds a pre-entry filter for the scanner/auto-rotation flow.

- Smart Entry Timing avoids chasing candles at the edge of a recent range or extreme single-candle moves.
- Correlation Guard limits the system to one selected position and reports when multiple markets are moving in the same direction.
- Dashboard shows timing and correlation state in Indonesian.
- These filters do not guarantee profit and do not activate LIVE orders.
- Paper testing remains the intended validation stage.
