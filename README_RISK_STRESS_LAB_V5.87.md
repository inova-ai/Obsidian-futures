# Obsidian Futures V5.87 — Risk Stress Lab

V5.87 adds a simulation-only stress layer over the V5.86 Full Trading Simulator.

Scenarios: baseline, cost x2/x3, execution shock, gap/slippage shock, funding stress, and worst-sequence ordering.

The stress lab does not modify the Paper/Simulator account and never sends real orders. It is a robustness analysis, not a profit forecast.
