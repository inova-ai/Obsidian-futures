# V5.80–V5.82 Market Intelligence

- V5.80 Session Intelligence: paper trade statistics by UTC market session.
- V5.81 Volatility/Event Filter: OHLC/ATR anomaly filter; it is not a news feed.
- V5.82 Confidence Calibration 2.0: confidence buckets, calibration MAE and Brier score.

All modules are observational and do not send real orders or automatically modify trading parameters.
